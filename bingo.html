VA Life Bingo

Merry & bright · Register · Match the pattern · Shout Bingo

Get your bingo card

Type your name and unwrap your own one-of-a-kind card. No two guests get the same card.

How to play

Register your name to unwrap your own card, then play along as the host draws ornaments. Each round is quick and uses a different winning pattern.

  1. Register. Type your name and tap Unwrap my card. You get a one-of-a-kind card.
  2. Watch the ornament draw. The host draws a VA experience, such as “Handled client calls”.
  3. Mark your square. Tap the matching square on your card. Only called squares can be marked.
  4. Shout Bingo. Complete this round’s pattern (shown above your card) and the BINGO button lights up. Tap it and the host checks your card.

Winning patterns

The host uses a different pattern each round. Each one needs only a few squares, so rounds take minutes.
