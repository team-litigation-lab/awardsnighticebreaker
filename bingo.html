How to play

Every card on the wall hides a different mix of VA moments behind its mask. Pick one, unmask it under your name, and play along as the host calls.

  1. Pick a mask. Choose any open card on the Card Wall. Once it is yours, nobody else can take it.
  2. Listen for the call. The host calls out a VA experience, such as “Handled client calls”.
  3. Mark your square. Tap the matching square on your card. Only called squares can be marked.
  4. Shout Bingo. Five in a row (across, down, or diagonal) lights up the BINGO button. Tap it and the host checks your card.
