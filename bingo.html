How to play

Every wrapped gift on the wall hides a different mix of VA moments behind its mask. Unwrap one under your name, turn your Bells on if you like, and play along as the host draws ornaments.

  1. Unwrap a gift. Pick any wrapped card on the Gift Wall. Once it is yours, nobody else can take it.
  2. Watch the ornament draw. The host draws a VA experience, such as “Handled client calls”.
  3. Mark your square. Tap the matching square on your card. Only called squares can be marked.
  4. Shout Bingo. Five in a row (across, down, or diagonal) lights up the BINGO button. Tap it and the host checks your card.
